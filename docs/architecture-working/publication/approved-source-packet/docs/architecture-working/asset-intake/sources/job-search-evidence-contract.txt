# Evidence Contract

## Binding repository contracts

This reference operationalizes, and does not replace, the approved industry
contact-discovery and work-team-company triangulation designs. The root
`AGENTS.md` and those designs control any conflict.

## Source capabilities

| Source | May establish | Does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current company people or research page | Displayed current role and formal membership | Private reporting line, hiring authority, or willingness to talk |
| Current company author page | Displayed role and named company work | Formal research-team membership |
| Paper, report, standard, patent, dataset, or benchmark | Authorship, named contribution, and technical subject | Current employer |
| Repository history | Direct implementation, review, or maintenance contribution | Current team, manager, or authority |
| Official job page or applicant-tracking API | Live role, mission, department, and displayed location | The person who controls hiring |
| Google Scholar, OpenAlex, DBLP, Crossref, or arXiv | Publication discovery and identity clues | Current affiliation without current corroboration |
| Public LinkedIn or professional profile | Supporting public identity or displayed role | Safe automated access, consent, or a sole proof when a stronger current source exists |
| General search result | A route to an underlying source | Evidence from the snippet alone |

## Evidence labels

- `Verified`: directly supported by a named current source or command result.
- `Likely`: inferred from verified evidence. Name the inference and missing
  check.
- `Assumption`: unverified and load-bearing. Name the source or action that
  would resolve it.

Company-level research-to-production conclusions remain `Likely` when all
evidence is under company custody. Use `Verified` only when a distinct primary
record or custodian corroborates the conclusion.

## Person evidence

Every formal roster row needs a current identity source, current-role source,
observation date, and work state. Work state is `verified`, `likely`,
`not_found`, `source_limited`, or `blocked`.

Every proposed contact candidate needs current identity, current affiliation,
and attributable-work evidence. If one is missing, use `deferred`. Never admit
by title, prestige, publication adjacency, or network warmth alone.

## Team and company evidence

Connect one current work item, personally attributable team evidence, and one
production surface. Keep formal and adjacent teams separate. Record a research
owner, implementation peer, manager, or route person only when a source
establishes that function. `not_found` is valid and does not mean no such person
exists.

## Currentness and conflicts

Record an observation date for each structured evidence row. Preserve
conflicting role or affiliation claims with separate sources and dates. A
blocked source, incomplete pagination, parser drift, or changing job board
creates a coverage limit. It does not prove absence.

## Contact data and privacy

Record an email only when a source publishes that exact address. Put
`Published email` and the source URL on the same report line. Do not infer email
patterns, mutual connections, private relationship facts, reporting lines,
hiring authority, or consent. Do not store credentials, cookies, private
messages, or private connection exports in a company report.

## Storage and action boundary

`RESEARCH-CONTACTS.md` is the only person store. Reports are assessment views.
Stop before applying a proposed ledger diff. Expose no sending, application,
publishing, introduction-request, or LinkedIn-automation action.
