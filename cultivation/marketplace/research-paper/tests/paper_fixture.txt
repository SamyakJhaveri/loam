Key-value stores serve most reads from memory, and the choice
of eviction policy decides how often a request must go to disk.
Operators tune that choice by hand, one deployment at a time.
We present E XAMPLE S YS, a trace-driven tuner that picks an
eviction policy for each workload from a short sample of its
requests. On the synthetic traces we use, the tuner’s choice
lowers the median read latency in most runs we measured, and
because the sample is replayed offline it never touches the
live store. E XAMPLE S YS compares every candidate policy with
a fixed replay harness, and we report each comparison with
the number of runs behind it. The tuner, the traces and the
scripts that produce every figure are released with the paper.
