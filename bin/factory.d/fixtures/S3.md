## Approach
`seed/.claude/settings.json` carries the exact deny list and sandbox block from `DESIGN.md` L0: no allow, no ask, no defaultMode, two hooks only.
Under `seed/.claude/hooks/`, delete 13 and keep `fable-session-brief.sh` and `post-compact-reinject.sh`.
Under `seed/.codex/`, delete `hooks.json` and `hooks/pre-tool-policy.py`, add `rules/loam.rules` with about 14 forbidden lines, and add the new `config.toml` with `approval_policy = "never"`, one profile extending `:workspace`, `.env*` deny globs, network enabled, no `sandbox_mode` key, and `default_permissions` above the first table.
In `bin/`, delete `verify-template.sh`, `verify-template-stages.sh`, `harness-smoke-stages.sh`, `verification_snapshot.py`, `rendered_harness_contract.py`, and `agent_parity/`; rewrite `bin/release.sh` under 40 lines; add `bin/hygiene`.
In `bin/tests/`, add `test_render_smoke.py` and delete the seven modules the spec names.
Every deletion travels with its importers and tests in the same commit, so `grep -rn` the module name first.
The done check `no-verify-template-refs` conflicts with the out-of-scope lines above, so this ticket also owns these six minimal edits, and nothing else outside them:
the one comment line in `bin/check` that names `verify-template-stages.sh`, comment only;
the two `assertNotIn("verify-template", ...)` guards in `bin/tests/test_ci_configuration.py`, which may be replaced by guards that do not spell the retired name;
the comment lines in `bin/lib.sh` that name a deleted script;
`bin/harness-smoke.sh`, deleted, since it wraps `harness-smoke-stages.sh`;
`seed/.codex/rules/default.rules`, deleted, since the DELETE block of spec commit 2 names it;
and the ignore lines in `seed/.gitignore.jinja` for artefacts of deleted hooks.
If the session cannot finish in one context, use the S3a and S3b split recipe in the Risks section of `.superpowers/lean-v3/04-SESSION-PLAN.md`; the naive split is not green-separable.
